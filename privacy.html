Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026 · Applies to supplydesk.in and the SupplyDesk mobile app

Who we are

SupplyDesk (“we”, “us”) is a B2B procurement platform. SupplyDesk manages sourcing between buyers and manufacturers: buyers deal only with SupplyDesk, and suppliers deal only with SupplyDesk.

Information we collect

  • Account details: name, business email address, phone number, company name, country.
  • Business details: product requirements, quantities, delivery locations, purchase orders, quotations, invoices, payment references and messages sent through SupplyDesk.
  • Supplier verification documents: business registration, certifications and similar files you upload.
  • Technical data: device and browser type, IP address, and pages viewed, used to keep the service secure and working.
  • Push notification token (mobile app, only if you allow notifications).

How we use it

  • To verify your email by one-time code and keep you signed in.
  • To process requirements, orders, invoices and payments, and to relay messages.
  • To verify suppliers and maintain the quality of listings.
  • To send service emails and notifications about your orders.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, and to comply with law (including tax and accounting rules).

Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. Buyer contact details are not shared with suppliers, and supplier contact details are not shared with buyers, unless SupplyDesk management explicitly approves a disclosure. We share data with service providers that help us run the platform (hosting, email delivery, and logistics or inspection partners for a specific order) and with authorities when the law requires it.

Retention

We keep account and transaction records for as long as your account is active and for the period required by tax, accounting and commercial law. Verification documents are kept while the supplier relationship continues and as needed for compliance.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS. Uploaded documents are stored in private storage and are available only to the people who need them. Access to the admin tools is restricted by role and every sensitive action is logged.

Your choices and rights

  • You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information.
  • Delete your account: sign in and use Delete my account on your dashboard (Buyer: bottom of the dashboard; Supplier: Company Details tab). Your personal and business contact data, login and uploaded documents are erased immediately. Orders, invoices and payment records are kept in anonymised form for the period required by tax and accounting law. Accounts with open orders or unsettled payments must settle them first; you can also email support@supplydesk.in from your registered address.
  • You can turn off push notifications in your phone settings at any time.

Children

SupplyDesk is for businesses. It is not directed to children under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for significant changes, notify account holders.

Contact

Questions or requests: support@supplydesk.in
